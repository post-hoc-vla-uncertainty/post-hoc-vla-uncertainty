Detection is not harm

Points spread across the whole plane. Some perturbations are detected but harmless: image noise, the occluder, the table texture. Others are harmful but barely visible to the heads; the clearest case is a familiar instruction from another task in the same scene.

The heads measure how well the training data support an input, not whether the action will succeed. This matches the paper's framing and is the reason E2 measures usefulness separately.

E2 · Referral within a task

Each condition (10 tasks × 10 starting states × 13 label-preserving variants, three of them expected harmless) is played three times by the primary and by a supervisor trained with label-preserving augmentation. The score of its starting observation decides which conditions go to the supervisor. Within-task nAUC: 0 = random referral, 1 = oracle. Mean over three seeds ± SD, hierarchical-bootstrap interval in brackets. Metrics computed with the paper's own analysis code.
